Questions, answered

Is it free?

Yes. RecipeFinder is free to download, and you can save as many recipes as you like.

Does it work on iPhone and iPad?

Yes, RecipeFinder runs on both iPhone and iPad, with layouts made for each screen.

Can I bring my recipes from Paprika, Mela or a spreadsheet?

Yes. Export from Paprika or Mela, or save a CSV file, then open Settings → Data Management → Import from Another App. Your recipes come across with their photos, and anything already in your library is skipped.

Where are my recipes kept?

On your device. Your recipes, meal plans and shopping lists stay with you, and you can save a full backup to a file you own whenever you like. Read more in the Privacy Policy.

Does it work offline?

Yes. Your library, meal plan, shopping list and cooking mode all work without a connection. Saving a recipe from a link or searching the web uses the internet while you do it.
