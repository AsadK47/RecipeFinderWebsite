Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 November 2026

1. Introduction

RecipeFinder, operated by Asad Khan, is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains how we handle your information when you use our website and mobile application.

2. Information We Collect

The App is private by default. The current version needs no account, has no analytics or advertising, and does not send your recipes, profile, plans or lists to us — they are stored on your device (and, if you turn on iCloud sync, in your own iCloud account).

iCloud sync. You can turn on iCloud sync in Settings > Data Management. It is off until you turn it on. While it is on, your recipes (including ingredients, instructions, notes, tags, cuisine, dietary information, favourites and the original text a recipe was imported from), photos you add to recipes, meal plans, shopping lists (including shop names), kitchen inventory, collections, cooking history, ratings, achievements and your profile name, chef type and photo are stored in the private database of your own iCloud account, using Apple's CloudKit service under Apple's terms and privacy policy, and kept up to date on each device signed in to that account. Your app preferences stay on your device, and recipe pictures loaded from websites are fetched again on each device rather than synced. We cannot access your iCloud account or the data in it.

You can turn iCloud sync off at any time in the same place; your data stays on your device. Data already stored in iCloud stays in your iCloud account until you remove it, which you can do in your device's iCloud settings. While sync is on, deleting data in the App, including through Reset, deletes it on every device signed in to your iCloud account. Reset keeps your iCloud sync choice. If you turn sync off, make changes (including deletions) and later turn it back on, those changes are then applied on every device signed in to your iCloud account.

Future versions may offer sharing recipes and collections with people you choose. Content you choose to share will be visible to the people you share it with. Any such feature will be off until you turn it on, and this policy will be updated to describe it before it is released.

Web searches and the pages you open from them load directly from the search engine or website you choose; browsing data from them is not kept.

This website collects only:

  • Email Address: If you sign up for launch notifications
  • Cookies: To remember your cookie preference

3. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

  • Send launch notifications (if you opted in)
  • Respond to your inquiries

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We share data only when required by law.

5. Your Rights (UK GDPR)

You have the right to access, correct, delete, or port your data. You may also object to processing or withdraw consent at any time.

6. Data Security

We implement appropriate security measures to protect your information.

7. Contact

For privacy concerns or data requests: recipefinderuk@gmail.com
