Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 November 2026

1. Introduction

RecipeFinder is operated by Asad Khan, an individual based in England and Wales ("we", "us", "our"). This policy explains how your information is handled when you use the RecipeFinder mobile app (the "App") and this website. The App's own Privacy Policy (in the App under Settings) says the same about the App.

2. Our Approach to Privacy

The App is designed to be private by default. In the current version, information you enter or generate is stored on your device (and, if you turn on iCloud sync, in your own iCloud account) and is not collected by, or accessible to, us, except where the App retrieves content from the internet for features you use (see Section 5) and feedback you choose to send while testing a beta version (see Beta testing in Section 6).

We will only collect or share personal information in the ways described in this policy. If we introduce a feature that would handle your information differently, we will update this policy before that feature is made available, and any such feature will be optional.

3. Accounts

The current version of the App does not require an account or sign-in. You can use its features without providing any personal information. Any name or photo you add to your profile is stored on your device (and in your own iCloud account if you turn on iCloud sync — see Section 7).

4. Information Stored on Your Device

The following is created and held on your device (and, if you turn on iCloud sync, in your own iCloud account — see Section 7):

  • Recipes you create or import, including ingredients, instructions, notes, tags, cuisine, dietary information, favourites and the original text a recipe was imported from
  • Meal plans, shopping lists (including shop names) and kitchen inventory
  • Collections, cooking history, ratings and achievements
  • Photos you add to recipes, together with your profile name, chef type and any profile photo

The following is held on your device:

  • Your app preferences and settings
  • Your recent web searches, so you can repeat them
  • The dishes you passed on in Plan my week, kept for 30 days so your next week's cards lead with something fresh
  • The order you usually shop the aisles in, for each shop you choose, and for trips with no shop chosen, learned from your ticks in I'm in the shop, so your list follows your route
  • Copies of pictures from recipe websites, so imported recipes show their picture offline
  • Up to three safety copies of your recipe database, made before an app update changes how it is stored, to help recover your recipes if an update goes wrong. Newer copies replace older ones after later updates, and they are kept out of device backups.
  • If your recipe database can't be opened, a copy of it set aside so it can be recovered

Reset (Settings > Data Management) clears the information in the first list above, your recent web searches, the dishes you passed on in Plan my week, the aisle order learned in I'm in the shop, the copies of website pictures and your preferences (your iCloud sync choice is kept), and restores the sample recipes. The safety copies and set-aside database copies stay until you uninstall the App.

Your device's own backups (iCloud Backup or a computer) include this information, except the safety copies, and are managed by Apple or your computer.

5. Network Activity

Recipe import. When you import a recipe by supplying a URL (directly or via the share sheet), the App retrieves content directly from the website you specify. We do not intermediate, store copies of, or monitor those requests. What you type in a web search is sent to the search engine you choose (change it in Settings > Recipe Search) and is handled under that engine's privacy policy. Web searches and the pages you open from them load directly from the search engine or website you choose, in a private browsing session that is cleared when you close the search. Your recent searches are kept on your device so you can repeat them; Reset clears them.

Images. When a recipe you import has a picture from a website, the App downloads it directly from that address in the background (on each of your devices, if iCloud sync is on) and keeps a copy on your device so the picture shows offline. No information about you is sent to those sources beyond what is required to request the image.

On-device processing. Where supported, recipe text interpretation (using Apple's on-device foundation models) and photo text recognition (using Apple's Vision framework) run entirely on your device. The text and photos you process are not sent to us or any third party. To offer to import a copied recipe link, the App asks iOS whether your clipboard holds a web link, which does not reveal its contents; it reads the clipboard when you tap Paste.

The App does not integrate with any third-party analytics services, advertising networks, or tracking technologies, and does not use advertising identifiers.

6. Apple Services

In-app purchases. If the App offers optional paid features, purchases are processed by Apple under Apple's terms and privacy policy. We do not receive or store your payment card details.

Notifications. The App may schedule local notifications (such as cooking timers and meal reminders) on your device, only with your permission.

Ratings. The App may ask you to rate it using Apple's standard prompt. No information is collected through this prompt.

Beta testing. If you test a beta version through Apple's TestFlight, feedback you choose to send (your comment, any screenshots and any crash report) reaches us through Apple, under Apple's TestFlight terms and privacy policy. Apple includes the time you sent it, your email address where Apple shares it, and technical details about your device such as its model, iOS version, language, time zone, network type, battery level and free storage. We use this to fix and improve the App.

Your comment (its opening words become the issue title), the time you sent it, and your device model and iOS version are posted as an issue in the App's public GitHub project, where anyone can read them. Your Apple account name and email address are kept out of the issue. Screenshots and crash reports are attached to the project's automated run for 14 days, where anyone signed in to GitHub can download them, and stay in App Store Connect under Apple's retention. Comments, screenshots and crash reports are public, so please keep personal details out of them.

Feedback is stored on GitHub (GitHub, Inc.) and read by us and by AI coding tools we use to investigate and fix issues (currently Anthropic's Claude). Apple, GitHub and Anthropic may process it in the United States. To have a posted comment and its files removed, contact us (Section 14); copies others made while it was public, such as search engine caches, are outside our control.

7. Optional Sync and Sharing

iCloud sync. You can turn on iCloud sync in Settings > Data Management. It is off until you turn it on. Turning it on or off takes effect the next time you open the App. While it is on, everything described in the first list in Section 4, including photos you add to recipes, is stored in the private database of your own iCloud account, using Apple's CloudKit service under Apple's terms and privacy policy, and kept up to date on each device signed in to that account. Each device fetches its own copy of pictures from recipe websites (see Images in Section 5). We cannot access your iCloud account or the data in it.

You can turn iCloud sync off at any time in the same place; sync stops the next time you open the App, and your data stays on your device. Data already stored in iCloud stays in your iCloud account until you remove it, which you can do in your device's iCloud settings. While sync is on, deleting data in the App, including through Reset, removes it from your iCloud account and from every device signed in to it. Reset keeps your iCloud sync choice. If you turn sync off, make changes (including deletions) and later turn it back on, the App then sends those changes to your iCloud account and to every device signed in to it.

Sharing. Through the iOS share sheet you can share a recipe (as a recipe file or as text), your shopping list (as text) and a My Cookbook page (as a picture with a caption naming the dish, how many times you've made it and when). You can also save a backup file of your data wherever you choose (see Section 9). What you share goes to the people and apps you choose, who can see and keep it, and is handled from then on under their terms; the App does not send us a copy. Any future feature that shares content in a new way will be off until you turn it on, and this policy will be updated to describe it before it is released.

8. Data Security

Data held on your device is protected by your device's own security, and data held in iCloud by Apple's. We do not hold copies of it; beta feedback you choose to send is handled as described under Beta testing in Section 6. We recommend that you secure your device with a strong passcode, and keep device encryption enabled.

9. Your Rights and Control in the App

You stay in control of your data. You may at any time:

  • Export your recipes and other data via Settings > Data Management (photos are not included in the export)
  • Delete your data via Settings > Data Management
  • Remove all data from your device by uninstalling the App (data in your iCloud account is removed as described in the second paragraph of Section 7)

10. Data Protection Laws

The App is designed to comply with the UK General Data Protection Regulation (UK GDPR) and the Data Protection Act 2018. Because we do not collect or receive the information you enter in the App, we do not act as a data controller of that information for most purposes. For beta feedback you choose to send (Beta testing in Section 6), we are the data controller and rely on legitimate interests (fixing and improving the App); comments stay posted until you ask for them to be removed. If another feature changes this, this policy will set out the lawful basis, retention period and your rights for that feature.

For beta feedback, you can ask us for a copy of it, to correct or delete it, or to stop using it, by contacting us (Section 14). You can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk).

11. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed to children under the age of 13, and you should not use the App if you are under 13.

12. This Website

For this website, we are the data controller. It handles the following information:

  • Launch notifications: if you sign up, your email address and the time you signed up are sent through Web3Forms, a form service that also receives your IP address and browser details to block spam, and delivered to our Gmail inbox.
  • Messages you send us: if you email us, your message and email address arrive in our Gmail inbox (Google).
  • Visiting the site: the site is hosted on GitHub Pages and delivered through Cloudflare, which receive your IP address and browser details when you visit, under their own privacy statements.
  • Your theme and cookie choice: saved in your browser's local storage and kept on your device; see the Cookie Policy.

We rely on your consent to send you launch notifications; you can withdraw it at any time by emailing us. We rely on legitimate interests to reply to messages you send and to run the site. We keep your sign-up email until we've told you RecipeFinder has launched, or until you ask us to remove it if that's sooner, then delete it. We keep messages for as long as we need to deal with your enquiry. GitHub and Cloudflare keep visit logs for the periods in their own privacy statements. GitHub, Cloudflare, Google and Web3Forms may process this information outside the UK, including in the United States, under the safeguards in their own terms.

We use these service providers to run the website and handle email, and we share information where the law requires it. We do not sell your personal information. You can ask us for a copy of your information, to correct or delete it, to restrict how we use it, to move it, or to object to its use, by contacting us (Section 14). You can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk). We protect your information with appropriate security measures.

13. Changes to This Policy

We may amend this policy at any time. The date of the most recent revision is shown at the top of this policy. Material changes will be notified through the App (and, for this website, on this page) before they take effect. Where a change relies on your consent, we will ask for it rather than treat continued use as consent.

14. Contact

For privacy concerns or data requests: Asad Khan, recipefinderuk@gmail.com, recipefinder.uk
