Cookie Policy

Last updated: 25 November 2026

1. What We Store

This website's own pages set no cookies. They save two small preferences in your browser's local storage, a similar store on your device, so the site remembers how you like it. They stay on your device.

2. How We Use It

Local storage remembers your theme (light or dark) and your choice on the cookie banner. If you decline, your theme isn't saved.

3. What Is Saved

Type Purpose Duration
Preferences Remember your theme and consent choices Until you clear your browser data

4. Managing These Preferences

You can clear these preferences at any time through your browser settings (clear site data for recipefinder.uk). Without them, the site follows your device's light or dark setting.
